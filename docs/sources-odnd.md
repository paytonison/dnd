## Naming reference inspected

The local, ignored `Dungeons & Dragons - White Box.pdf` was visually inspected for this requirement. Its Volume I, *Men & Magic*, title page identifies Gary Gygax and Dave Arneson and carries a 1974 copyright. The file appears to be a digitally retypeset compilation, with 2016 PDF metadata; its exact original printing and compilation provenance have not been established. Treat it as provisional naming evidence, not a verified facsimile of the first printing.
