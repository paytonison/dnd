# 5E (2014) / SRD 5.1 source ledger

Documentation for **Dungeoning a Dragon v1.0.0**, the basic, minimum application. See [the release policy](versioning.md).

The user selected **5E** for the original 2014 rules and **5.5E** for the revised 2024 rules on September 11, 2026. The new `srd51` module and `srd51-core` pack are separate from the existing `srd55` identities. SRD **5.1** is a publication version; module and pack **1.0.0** are implementation versions. Neither changes the shared character-file format or converts an existing character to another edition.

This first implementation slice is **experimental and partial**: ordinary Human, Fighter levels 1-3, Champion at level 3, and Acolyte. The original Fighter progresses to level 20; level 3 is an implementation boundary. The supported source profile includes the 2014 rules' later corrections evidenced by the 2018 Basic Rules. It does not import 2024 mechanics.

## Independently derived acceptance examples

These expectations were calculated from the cited rules, independently of the evaluator. They specify what regression checks should establish; this ledger does not itself report an executed test, native session, PDF-export check, or packaged application acceptance.

- **Human Fighter/Acolyte baseline:** before racial increases, assign Strength 15, Dexterity 14, Constitution 13, Intelligence 8, Wisdom 12, Charisma 10. Human totals are **16/15/14/9/13/11**, giving modifiers **+3/+2/+2/-1/+1/+0**. This is the standard array; its point-buy cost is also 9 + 7 + 5 + 0 + 4 + 2 = **27**.
- **HP progression:** first-level maximum is 10 + 2 = **12**; each fixed gain is 6 + 2 = **8**, producing **12/20/28** at levels 1/2/3. Accepted gains of 1 and 10 instead produce 12 + (1 + 2) + (10 + 2) = **27** at level 3.
- **Armor and skill contributions:** chain mail 16 + shield 2 + Defense 1 = **AC 19**, with no Dexterity contribution. Strength 16 meets chain mail's 13 threshold, retaining **30 feet** speed. Athletics is 3 + 2 = **+5**; Perception and Acolyte Insight are 1 + 2 = **+3**; Acolyte Religion is -1 + 2 = **+1**.
- **Style-specific attacks:** a longsword attack uses Strength 3 + proficiency 2 = **+5**. With Dueling and one handed use, damage is **1d8 + 3 + 2 = 1d8+5**; the shield is compatible with that style. Archery with a longbow uses Dexterity 2 + proficiency 2 + style 2 = **+6**, damage **1d8+2**. A dart is a ranged finesse weapon: selecting Strength yields 3 + 2 + 2 = **+7** under Archery. A thrown handaxe remains a melee weapon and receives no Archery bonus.
- **Negative Constitution:** accepted base Constitution 3 becomes Human Constitution 4, modifier **-3**. First HP is 10 - 3 = **7**. A later accepted d10 result of 1 grants max(1, 1 - 3) = **1**, producing **8** at level 2. A short-rest d10 result of 1 instead heals max(0, 1 - 3) = **0** while still spending that die. These two floors are different rules.
- **Resources and recovery:** at Fighter levels 1/2/3, Second Wind capacities are **1/1/1** and Action Surge capacities **0/1/1**. A level-3 Second Wind die of 4 restores **7 HP**, limited by missing HP. A qualifying long rest with zero unspent Hit Dice at level 3 restores max(1, floor(3 / 2)) = **1 Hit Die**, not all three.
- **Pricing:** buying the published 20-bullet bundle from Acolyte's 15 gp leaves 1,500 - 4 = **1,496 cp**. A 1 cp-per-bullet price would charge 20 cp for the same bundle and is not source-equivalent.

## Remaining boundaries

Other races/classes/backgrounds, other Fighter archetypes, levels 4-20, feats and variant Human, multiclassing, spellcasting, exotic languages, starting-gold alternatives, magic items, general inventory transactions, containers, encumbrance, and encounter adjudication remain unimplemented in this slice. Accepted rolls, resources, action history, overrides, disabled/missing packs, exact-version saves, and imports must still be checked through the public application contracts. Source inspection and mechanically accurate sample calculations do not establish those acceptance gates or complete SRD 5.1 coverage.

The source audit was performed against the in-progress 5E additions on top of repository revision `f6686554fca278dd15ecea6f17495dbe1a6f3d59`. The new module/pack and supporting edits were uncommitted; this revision alone does not contain them. Current automated, native, export and distribution evidence must identify the actual working-tree artifact separately.
